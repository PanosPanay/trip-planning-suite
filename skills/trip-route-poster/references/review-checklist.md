# Route Poster Review Checklist

- Real coordinates and route geometry; guide lines never masquerade as roads.
- Solid default route, dashed optional branches, clear endpoint labels and legend.
- Every white callout matches a numbered marker and uses a short leader line where displaced.
- Place names, altitude, stay day, distance, and time fit inside their boxes.
- Stay badges do not hide day or point numbers.
- Labels and distance chips avoid route strokes unless a tiny overlap is explicitly accepted.
- Optional branch labels sit near their own endpoints and remain distinguishable.
- Insets show ordered movement, including entrance/shuttle/first stop where relevant.
- Header highlights the real flagship sights; day titles are specific rather than generic.
- Empty map space is cropped and useful inset/detail space is enlarged.
- Landscape and portrait HTML are reviewed before export.
- Export uses a fixed canvas and device scale 2 or higher; no stitched full-page screenshot.
- Final PNGs are opened and inspected after capture.
