# Hotel Verification Fields

Open the exact room offer and record: stay dates, room name, bed count/size, rooms available, occupancy, total nightly price and taxes, breakfast, prepayment, free-cancellation deadline, cancellation penalty, no-show rule, invoice, latest arrival, opening/renovation year source, rating/review count, parking/transit, heating or oxygen where relevant, and direct booking URL.

Do not store cookies, login tokens, or the persistent browser profile in a trip project or shared package.
